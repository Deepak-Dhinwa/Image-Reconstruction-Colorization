Reconstruction and Color

One frozen DINOv2 encoder, adapted with LoRA, feeds two heads: fill the missing image, and guess the color.

DINOv2LoRACOCO 2017

Question

  • Can a shared vision backbone reconstruct a damaged image and colorize a gray one without two separate networks?

Method

  • DINOv2 ViT-S/14 stays frozen. LoRA rank 32 is what trains.
  • A shared decoder splits into an RGB reconstruction head and a Lab color head.
  • Trained on COCO 2017. The saved weights are model_BEST_smallgpu.pth.

Evidence

2epochs completed
5,000COCO val images on disk
5comparison images in results/

What the result actually says

  • DINOv2 ViT-S/14 stays frozen. LoRA rank 32, scale 32, is what trains, plus the last transformer block. That is what fits a 4 GB RTX 3050.
  • Reconstruction loss is 0.8 L1 plus 0.2 times one minus SSIM. Color is a separate Lab head on the same decoder.
  • Five comparison images are in results/. Reconstruction holds structure. Color is only partly learned.

What it does not show

  • Training stopped at 2 epochs, batch size 1, with gradient accumulation. The report is explicit that color needs something like 20 epochs.
  • The best weights, model_BEST_smallgpu.pth, are 97 MB and stay on this machine. GitHub has the code and the five sample images, not the checkpoint.

Beside the other attempts

  • The two heads share the encoder and the decoder, then split. Reconstruction is the head that became usable. Colorization is the head that did not.
  • COCO 2017 validation is 5,000 images on disk. Training images are the COCO train split, also local.

Stack

PyTorch 2.5.1 with CUDA, DINOv2, LoRA. Environment is recon-color_env. Do not upgrade it. The checkpoint it matches is model_BEST_smallgpu.pth.
